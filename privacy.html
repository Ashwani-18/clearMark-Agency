Privacy Policy

ClearMark Agency ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described here.

Data We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide: When you fill in our contact form, we collect your name, business name, phone number, Google Maps link, and the message you send.
  • Communication data: If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we may retain records of those communications to manage your enquiry.
  • Service data: During service delivery, we may receive access to your Google Business Profile data (review content, rating, profile information) solely for the purpose of providing the agreed service.
  • Website usage data: We use basic analytics to understand how visitors use this site (page visits, referral source, device type). No personally identifiable information is collected at this level.

We do not use cookies for advertising, tracking, or profiling purposes.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and deliver the services you have requested
  • Send you reports and updates related to your active service engagement

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing. We will not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Google: When reporting reviews, we submit information through Google's official flagging and support channels. This is necessary to deliver the service.
  • Legal authorities: Where required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at +91 94580 12247 or via the contact form. We will respond within 30 days.
