Service 01

Google Review Reporting & Removal

Fake reviews, spam, competitor sabotage — they do happen, and they damage businesses that have done nothing wrong. Google's policies exist to prevent this, but navigating the reporting process correctly takes time and knowledge.

We audit your existing reviews, identify those that appear to violate Google's content policies, and submit properly structured reports through Google's official channels. For cases that require it, we escalate to Google Business Support directly.

Important: We can only report reviews that violate Google's stated policies. Honest negative reviews from real customers cannot and should not be removed — we will tell you this directly if that is the case.

Start With a Free Audit

What This Covers

  • Full review audit across your Google Business Profile
  • Identification of reviews violating Google policies (spam, fake, off-topic, conflict of interest)
  • Structured flagging reports submitted through official Google channels
  • Escalation to Google Business Support for unresolved cases
  • Legal escalation guidance for defamatory content
  • Monthly status updates on all active reports

What This Does Not Cover

  • Removal of genuine negative reviews from real customers
  • Guaranteed removal — Google makes the final decision
  • Any methods that violate Google's own policies

Service 02

Automated Review Request Campaigns

Most satisfied customers don't leave reviews. Not because they had a bad experience, but because no one asked. A well-timed, well-worded ask changes that — and it's something Google explicitly allows.

We set up automated follow-up sequences — SMS, email, or WhatsApp — that invite your real customers to share their experience. The sequences are sent to all customers, not filtered by predicted sentiment, which keeps them within Google's guidelines.

Set Up Your Campaign

What This Covers

  • Custom follow-up message sequences (2–3 touchpoints)
  • Delivery via SMS, email, or WhatsApp — your choice
  • Google-policy-compliant messaging (no incentives, no filtering)
  • Direct link to your Google review form
  • Monthly reporting: send volume, open rates, review conversion
  • A/B testing of message timing and copy

What You Need to Provide

  • Customer contact list (name + phone or email)
  • Approximate timing of their most recent visit or purchase

Service 03

Professional Review Response Writing

How a business responds to reviews — positive and negative — is one of the first things potential customers read. A generic "thank you for your feedback" response signals that no one is home. A thoughtful, specific response signals a business that cares.

We write personalised responses to your Google reviews on a rolling basis. Each response is written in your brand's voice, acknowledges the specific feedback, and — for negative reviews — addresses concerns without being defensive or making promises you can't keep.

Start Responding Better

What This Covers

  • Monthly response writing for all new reviews (positive and negative)
  • Voice and tone brief developed with you in onboarding
  • Personalised responses — no copy-paste templates
  • Negative review handling: calm, factual, solution-focused
  • Responses posted directly to your profile (manager access required)
  • Review summary report included monthly

Response Turnaround

  • Negative reviews: within 24 hours
  • Positive reviews: within 48 hours

Service 04

Custom Website Design & Local SEO

A strong Google Business Profile needs a credible website behind it. When someone sees your reviews and clicks through, what they find either confirms their interest or loses it in seconds.

We design and build custom websites for local businesses — not templates with your logo dropped in. Pages are built with local search in mind: semantic HTML, correct schema markup, fast load times, and content structured around the searches your customers actually make.

Discuss Your Website

What This Covers

  • Custom design (no page-builder templates)
  • Mobile-first, responsive across all screen sizes
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile integration, local schema markup
  • On-page SEO: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure
  • Performance optimisation: target Lighthouse score 90+
  • Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation
  • Contact forms and click-to-call links
  • Full handover with all source files and credentials

Typical Delivery

  • 3–5 page site: 2–3 weeks
  • Larger sites: scoped individually
