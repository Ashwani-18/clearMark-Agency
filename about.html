About Us

We work within
the rules.

In a space full of agencies promising overnight transformations, we built ClearMark on a different premise: be honest about what we can and can't do, and do the work properly.

Our Mission

Give local businesses a fair shot at an honest reputation.

A single fake review can take days to remove and cost a business weeks of customer trust. Meanwhile, genuine customers who had excellent experiences rarely think to leave a review unprompted. The system isn't designed for busy business owners.

We exist to correct that imbalance — by doing the detail work that most businesses don't have time to learn: correctly structured policy reports, well-timed customer outreach, on-brand review responses, and websites built to rank where it counts.

Every step of our process operates within Google's published guidelines. We won't tell you a review will be removed unless we genuinely believe it violates policy. We won't inflate your review count with anything other than real customers. That's not a limitation — it's the point.

Honesty over hype

We tell you what's achievable before we start, and we tell you what actually happened at the end of every month.

Policy-first always

Everything we do is designed to work within Google's guidelines — not around them. Shortcuts aren't worth the risk to your profile.

No hidden methods

We'll explain exactly what we're doing and why. If you have questions, we answer them — in plain language, not marketing speak.

Your reputation is yours

At the end of every engagement, you own everything: your profile access, your review history, your website code. We don't hold things hostage.

Our Story

Started because a family business asked us for help.

ClearMark started simply. A relative's business — a small medical clinic that had served the community for twelve years — had been hit with a wave of clearly fake reviews. Their rating dropped from 4.5 to 3.1 in a month. New patients were calling to ask if they were still open.

We spent weeks learning how Google's flagging system actually works, how to write a report that gets read, and when to escalate. We got nine of eleven flagged reviews removed. The rating recovered. Appointments came back.

Other businesses started asking for the same help. We formalised the process, added review campaign management and response writing, then web design — because every business we helped with reviews eventually needed a better website too.

That's all ClearMark is: a small team that learned how to do this properly and decided to do it for others.

Our Approach

Detailed work, plain communication.

We read the guidelines.

Before we report a review, we verify it against Google's actual published content policies — not a summary of them. This means our reports are specific, well-structured, and more likely to be acted upon.

We write clearly.

Monthly reports tell you what happened, what it means, and what we're doing next. We don't dress up bad news and we don't overclaim good results.

We move fast.

Negative reviews get responses within 24 hours. Reports are filed the day we identify a violation. Your clients deserve to see a business that's paying attention.
