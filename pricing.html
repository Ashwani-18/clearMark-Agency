For Clarity

What every plan includes — and what it doesn't.

Included in all plans

  • Initial review audit at no charge
  • Monthly written report on all actions taken
  • Direct access to your account manager by WhatsApp
  • Full compliance with Google's content policies
  • Honest communication if a review doesn't qualify for removal
  • All credentials and access returned to you on cancellation

Not included in any plan

  • Guaranteed removal of any specific review
  • Fake or purchased reviews of any kind
  • Review gating (only inviting customers who will rate positively)
  • Any method that violates Google's policies
  • Legal action — we can flag; litigation is separate
