The clinic had operated for over ten years with a strong patient base, but their Google rating had been sitting at 3.2 stars — dragged down by a cluster of reviews that had appeared over a short period and bore the hallmarks of coordinated spam.

We audited all 47 reviews on their profile. Fourteen showed clear policy violations: identical phrasing across accounts, reviewer profiles with no prior activity, and reviews referencing services the clinic doesn't offer. We submitted structured reports through Google's official flagging system and followed up via Google Business Support for two that weren't actioned within the standard window.

Alongside removal, we set up a post-appointment SMS sequence inviting recent patients to share their experience on Google. Within 90 days, the clinic had 38 new authentic reviews. Their rating moved from 3.2 to 4.6 — reflecting what their patients had been telling them in person for years.

The restaurant had 23 Google reviews after two years of operation — fine food, good service, but almost invisible on local search. Without a steady stream of reviews, they weren't surfacing when people searched for dining options nearby.

We built a WhatsApp follow-up sequence triggered 24 hours after a customer's visit. The message was direct, friendly, and contained a single link to their Google review form. No promises, no incentives — just a polite ask from the restaurant owner.

Over 90 days, 83 customers left reviews. We also wrote personalised responses to every review — positive and negative — within 48 hours. The owner told us that two customers specifically mentioned the response to their review when they returned for a second visit.

The CA firm had a Google Business Profile with good reviews but a website that hadn't been updated since 2017. It loaded slowly on mobile, had no clear call to action, and didn't mention any of the specific services their clients searched for.

We rebuilt the site from scratch: custom design, fast-loading pages, service-specific landing pages targeting local keywords ("CA in Agra," "GST filing Agra"), proper LocalBusiness schema, and a prominent contact form on every page.

Within three months of launch, the site was ranking on page one for their primary service terms. Enquiries through the website increased by approximately three times compared to the previous year's same period. The firm has since expanded their team to handle the additional work.
